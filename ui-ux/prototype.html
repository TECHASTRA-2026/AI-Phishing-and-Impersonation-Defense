What we'll look for

Common patterns that can make a message worth double-checking.

Unusual urgency

Pressure to act immediately or face a consequence.

Sensitive information

Requests for passwords, OTPs, or payment details.

Suspicious links

Links that use unusual formats or unexpected destinations.

The safer approach

Never share a password or one-time code through a message link. Open the service's official app or type its known website yourself.
