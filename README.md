# AI-Phishing-and-Impersonation-Defense
With the increasing use of artificial intelligence, phishing attacks and digital impersonation are becoming more convincing and difficult to identify. Attackers can use AI-generated messages, cloned branding, fake identities, and manipulated communication to deceive individuals and organizations.

Traditional security systems may struggle to identify sophisticated attempts that imitate legitimate people, businesses, or institutions.

Challenge: Develop an AI-powered solution that can identify, analyze, and help prevent phishing attempts and digital impersonation across different communication channels while providing understandable explanations and actionable security recommendations.
